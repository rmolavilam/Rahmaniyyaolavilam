About

Rahmaniyya Madarasa Olavilam is an Islamic educational institution functioning under the guidance of Samastha Kerala Jam'iyyathul Ulama. Established on 9 November 1959, the madrasa has been committed to nurturing generations of students with authentic Islamic knowledge, strong moral values, and exemplary character. With classes from Class 1 to Class 11, the madrasa provides systematic Islamic education based on the Samastha curriculum. Our dedicated teachers strive to cultivate faith, discipline, good manners, and a lifelong love for learning while preserving the rich traditions of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah. Located in Olavilam, Chokli, Kannur, the madrasa remains devoted to its mission of delivering quality Islamic education with sincerity, excellence, and service to the Ummah.
